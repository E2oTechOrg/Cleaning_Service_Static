Cancellation & Refund Policy

How cancellations, rescheduling and refunds are handled.

Terms & Policies

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Rescheduling

You may reschedule a booking free of charge by informing us at least 24 hours before the agreed time. Rescheduling is subject to team availability.

2. Cancellation by You

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance carry no charge.
  • Late cancellations or no-shows may attract a reasonable charge to cover travel and team time, which we will explain to you.

3. Cancellation by Us

If we must cancel or delay due to weather, safety concerns or unforeseen circumstances, we will inform you as early as possible and offer a new date or a full refund of any advance paid.

4. Refunds

  • Advance payments for cancelled services are refunded as per the cancellation terms above.
  • Services already completed are not refundable, but see our Service & Quality Policy for re-cleaning of unsatisfactory work.

5. Refund Process

Approved refunds are returned to you by the same method used for payment, normally within a reasonable number of working days after approval.
